# Donation disclosure

Support is optional. Contributions do not unlock features and are not tax-deductible unless the maintainer expressly states otherwise and has verified that status. Configured payment links open a third-party provider, which handles payment and applies its own availability, fees, terms, and privacy policy. Private Story Reader does not collect card or bank details.

No payment account is configured in this repository. The maintainer must verify payout eligibility, platform fees, and legal/tax treatment for their country before publishing a destination. Do not add a donation link that belongs to someone else.
