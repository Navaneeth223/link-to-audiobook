# Donation disclosure

Private Story Reader is a free, independent, open-source project supported by voluntary donations. Gifts do not unlock features. Configured payment links open a third-party provider, which handles payment and applies its own availability, fees, terms, and privacy policy. Private Story Reader does not collect card or bank details. Contact: navaneethkv.dev@gmail.com.

This is a good-faith disclosure template, not legal or tax advice. Have a qualified lawyer review the project policies before public launch.

No payment account is configured in this repository. The maintainer must verify payout eligibility, platform fees, and legal/tax treatment for their country before publishing a destination. Do not add a donation link that belongs to someone else.
