# Community standards

We want participation to be respectful, constructive, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Examples of behavior that supports this community include clear and considerate feedback, accepting corrections, focusing criticism on ideas and code, and helping others reproduce problems without sharing private documents or credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate exposure of private data are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Report concerns privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for sensitive issues or contact a repository maintainer for other conduct concerns.
